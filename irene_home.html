Hey there, I’m Irene! Originally from Taiwan, I’ve spent years immersed in music,
but now I’m channeling my passion for creativity into graphic design.
Although I’m new to the field, I bring a musician’s eye for detail and a love for simplicity to every project.

For me, design is all about purpose
—each color, layout, and element is carefully chosen to create something meaningful, just like a piece of music.
I’m excited to bring my unique perspective to the world of design, combining fresh ideas with thoughtful execution.
